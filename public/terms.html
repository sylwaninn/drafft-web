Who we are and what you accept

drafft is a dating app for people who train, published by drafft. These terms are a contract between you and drafft. They apply to the drafft app and the getdrafft.com website, together with the privacy policy, which explains how your data is handled.

You accept them by creating your account. If you don’t accept them, don’t use drafft.

What drafft offers

drafft lets you create a profile built around the sports you do, discover profiles near you, like people, chat with the people you match with and propose a session to them: a sport, a day, a time.

drafft connects people. drafft doesn’t organise sessions, doesn’t attend them, and doesn’t check members’ identity, background or sporting level, apart from their phone number. A match shows mutual interest; it doesn’t promise a meeting.

Most features are free. Some extra features are paid: drafft tempo, boosts and super likes.

To use drafft

To create an account, you must:

  • be 18 or over;
  • be able to enter into a contract and not be barred from doing so by the law of the country you’re in;
  • never have been convicted of a sexual or violent offence;
  • not have been banned from drafft;
  • use your real identity and have only one account;
  • verify a mobile number that belongs to you.

Your account

The information you give must be accurate and up to date. Keep your password to yourself: anything done from your account is treated as done by you. If you think someone is using it without your permission, change your password and write to us.

You can pause your profile (You › Discover › Pause my profile): it no longer appears in Discover and your matches, chats and sessions stay. You can delete your account at any time (You › Delete account). Deleting your account or the app doesn’t cancel an active subscription: for that, see drafft tempo.

  • post hateful or discriminatory content, including on the grounds of origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or body;
  • send sexual content or nudity to someone who didn’t ask for it; profile photos and videos must contain no nudity or sexual content;

Meeting people and safety

You’re responsible for your interactions and meetings with other members. For a first session, meet in a public place, tell someone you trust and make sure you can get home on your own. You can leave a session at any time, no reason needed. You’ll find tips in the app (You › Help › Safety tips).

Train at your own level and according to your health. drafft gives no medical or training advice.

In an emergency, call the emergency services (112 in the European Union).

Report content

From a profile or a chat, tap Report or block. A report also blocks the person, ends the match and stays confidential: they don’t know who reported them.

Anyone, even without an account, can report content they believe is illegal by writing to support@getdrafft.com with: what the problem is and why, where the content is (profile, first name, screenshot), their name and email address, and a statement confirming the report is made in good faith. We acknowledge receipt and tell the person who reported the content what we decided.

This address is also drafft’s point of contact for authorities and members under the EU Digital Services Act. We reply in French or English.

Moderation and sanctions

Profile photos are checked automatically; unclear cases and every report are reviewed by a person on the team. Depending on how serious it is, drafft can reject a photo, delete a message, put an account on hold while it’s checked (a review or a selfie) or ban it permanently. Some signals, such as a link to a banned account, can put an account on hold automatically for that check. A report alone is never enough to put an account on hold, and a ban is always decided by a person.

When drafft makes a decision about you, you’re told about it and why, unless the law or someone’s safety prevents it. You can challenge it from the app’s help center or by writing to support@getdrafft.com: a different person will review your request. You keep the right to go to court.

Clearly illegal content may be passed on to the relevant authorities.

Boosts and super likes

They’re sold one at a time or in packs, don’t expire and are used up when you use them. They can’t be refunded in cash or transferred, and any left are lost if you delete your account.
