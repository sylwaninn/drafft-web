Privacy policy

What drafft knows about you, why, who it’s shared with, how long it’s kept and how you stay in control. This policy covers the drafft app and the getdrafft.com website.

In short

  • We collect what drafft needs to work: your account, your profile, an approximate area, your chats and your sessions.
  • Your exact location never leaves your phone: drafft only receives an area rounded to about 1 km. Your date of birth, phone number and email address are never shown to other members.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no advertising trackers. The app’s usage statistics are only collected with your consent. The website sets no cookies and counts its visits without identifying anyone.
  • Your data is stored in the European Union. A few providers are based in the United States: they’re listed below, with the safeguards that cover these transfers.
  • You can export your data or delete your account at any time from the app.

Contact

To exercise your rights or reach the data protection officer, write to support@getdrafft.com.

The data we collect

What you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored as a hash that can’t be read back) and mobile number, verified by SMS.
  • Profile: first name, date of birth, gender, the genders you want to see, neighbourhood or city, bio, goal, sports and how many times a week, answers to profile prompts, icebreaker, photos, voice intro, app language, notification settings and search filters (distance, age, sports).
  • Lifestyle, if you choose to fill it in: daily rhythm, diet, drinking, smoking. This step is optional.
  • Interactions: likes and super likes, with their note if any, profiles you pass, matches, messages (text, photos, videos, voice messages, reactions), session invites (sport, title if any, times, reply).
  • Safety: the reports and blocks you make, and a verification selfie, only if your account is put on hold for a check.
  • Help: the messages you send to the help center, their topic, the email address to reply to and a few details that help us help you (app version, the screen you wrote from, your account status, the purchase concerned).

What’s collected when you use drafft

  • Approximate area: with your permission, the app reads your location at reduced accuracy and rounds it on your phone to a grid of about 1 km before sending it. Your exact location never leaves your phone. To find the name of your city, this rounded area may be sent to your phone system’s maps service.
  • Device and connection: model, system and app versions, language, time zone, install identifier, how many times you opened the app and when first and last, IP address and the country it indicates, a device integrity token provided by the phone’s system and a notification token. The time zone is also used to send session reminders at the right time.
  • Activity: when you were last active, purchases and credits (boosts, super likes, drafft tempo). We never receive your card details: payments go through your phone’s app store.
  • Diagnostics: if the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report (device, versions, how the error happened, a pseudonymous account identifier) is sent so we can fix it. It contains neither your messages nor your photos.
  • Usage statistics, only if you agree: the screens you open and the actions you take in the app (for example opening a profile or proposing a session), linked to a pseudonymous identifier. No message content, no photos and no screen recording.

What your phone’s permissions are used for

Location is needed to show you profiles near you. Camera, microphone and photo library let you add photos, videos, voice messages or your voice intro, and take a verification selfie if we ask for one. The calendar is only used when you add a session: drafft creates the event, updates it or removes it if the session changes, and doesn’t use your other events. You can withdraw each permission in your phone’s settings. drafft doesn’t ask for your contacts, your health data or permission to track you.

Sensitive data

Your gender and the genders you want to see can reveal your sexual orientation. Your lifestyle answers (drinking, smoking, diet) can reveal information about your health or beliefs. Using a dating app is itself personal information.

This data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box for it, at sign-up or when the app asks, so drafft can show you profiles and show yours. You can withdraw it at any time: edit or clear your lifestyle answers in your profile, or delete your account (You › Privacy & data › Sensitive data consent). Your gender and the genders you want to see are needed for drafft to work: to stop their processing, delete your account.

Automated processing

  • Profiles shown in Discover: only profiles active in the last 30 days, with at least one approved photo, that match your filters (distance, age, sports) and whose preferred genders match both ways appear. People who sent you a super like come first, then boosted profiles near you, then everyone else, ranked by distance, recent activity, shared sports and whether someone has already liked you. This ranking has no legal effect and doesn’t deny you any service.
  • Photo checks: every profile photo is analysed automatically to detect nudity, violence or other prohibited content. It can be rejected automatically; unclear cases wait for a person to review them. If you think a rejection is a mistake, you can ask for a second look: a person does it. Photos and videos sent in a chat are analysed after they’re sent; anything unclear is flagged to moderation, with no automatic decision. Voice messages and the voice intro aren’t analysed.
  • Banned accounts coming back: encrypted fingerprints of the email, phone number and login identifiers, and a device integrity marker, help spot a banned account that returns. A banned identity can’t create a new account. A match can automatically put the account on hold while it’s reviewed or a verification selfie is checked. A ban is always decided by a person.
  • Phone number: we check the type of number; numbers that aren’t mobile numbers are refused.

What other members see

Other members see your profile: first name, age, neighbourhood or city, bio, goal, the lifestyle answers you gave, sports and how many times a week, prompt answers, icebreaker, approved photos and voice intro. In Discover, they also see the distance between you, rounded to the kilometre (1 km minimum). The people you match with also see when you’re online, your messages and your session invites. drafft tempo members see who liked them, with the note if any; others only see a blurred preview.

Never shown: your date of birth, email address, phone number and the genders you want to see. When you pause your profile, it no longer appears in Discover or in likes; your matches, chats and sessions stay.

Access by the drafft team

Only the team members who need it can access data, with rights limited to their role (help, moderation, administration). To handle a report, a help request, a risk to members’ safety or a request from an authority, moderation can read a match’s chat, delete a message that breaks the rules and view a verification selfie. Every access and every action is logged.

When a provider processes data outside the European Union, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework if the provider is certified under it, and otherwise by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. You can get a copy of these safeguards by writing to support@getdrafft.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, receive it in a reusable format (portability) and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

  • Export your data: You › Privacy & data › Export my data. You get one or more download links by email, usually within 24 hours, valid for 7 days.
  • Delete your account: You › Delete account. Deletion is immediate. Without the app: delete your account.
  • Edit your profile: at any time from the You tab. Your date of birth and gender can’t be changed after sign-up: write to us if there’s a mistake.
  • Accept or refuse usage statistics: the app asks you, and you can change your mind at any time in You › Privacy & data. Refusing doesn’t change anything you can do in drafft.
  • Anything else: write to support@getdrafft.com. We reply within one month at most. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you think your rights aren’t being respected, you can complain to the CNIL (Commission nationale de l’informatique et des libertés, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, cnil.fr) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Traffic between you and the app or website is encrypted (HTTPS). Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Each member can only access what concerns them. Photos, videos and voice messages are private and served through temporary links, valid for about an hour. Photos you send are stripped of their location metadata. Team access is limited and logged. No measure is foolproof: if a data breach puts you at risk, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Under 18

drafft is only for people aged 18 or over. Age is checked at sign-up and the date of birth can’t be changed afterwards. If you think someone under 18 is using drafft, report their profile: the team reviews the report and closes the account if the person is under 18.

Cookies and trackers

The getdrafft.com website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It counts its visits with PostHog, without identifying anyone: the page viewed, the site you came from, the campaign parameters of the link you followed, your type of browser and device and your country. To count visitors, PostHog uses a code made from your IP address and your browser, changed every day and never stored: nobody is followed from one day to the next, and your IP address isn’t kept. Your browser contacts no other site: the measurement passes through getdrafft.com, and its fonts are hosted on the site. It stays off if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Our host, Cloudflare, processes visitors’ IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, and keeps them for 7 days at most in its technical logs.

The app uses no advertising trackers. Usage statistics (PostHog) are only switched on with your consent, and crash reports (Sentry) are only used to fix errors. The app keeps on your phone what it needs to work: your login, your profile, your matches, your sessions, the latest messages of each chat, a copy of photos already shown and, during sign-up, your answers so far.
