Privacy policy

What drafft knows about you, why, who it’s shared with, how long it’s kept and how you stay in control. This policy covers the drafft app and the getdrafft.com website.

In short

  • We collect what drafft needs to work: your account, your profile, an approximate area, your chats and your sessions.
  • Your exact location never leaves your phone: drafft only receives an area rounded to about 1 km. Your date of birth, phone number and email address are never shown to other members.
  • No ads, no selling of data, no advertising trackers. The website sets no cookies.
  • Your data is stored in the European Union. A few providers are based in the United States: they’re listed below, with the safeguards that cover these transfers.
  • You can export your data or delete your account at any time from the app.

The data we collect

What you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored as a hash that can’t be read back) and phone number, verified by SMS.
  • Profile: first name, date of birth, gender, pronouns if you add them, the genders you want to see, neighbourhood, bio, goal, favourite spot, sports and how many times a week, answers to profile prompts, icebreaker, photos, video and voice intro, app language.
  • Lifestyle, if you choose to fill it in: daily rhythm, diet, drinking, smoking. This step is optional.
  • Interactions: likes, super likes and their notes, matches, messages (text, photos, videos, files, voice messages), session invites (sport, note, times, reply).
  • Safety: the reports and blocks you make and, only if moderation asks for it, a verification selfie.
  • Help: the messages you send to the help center and the email address to reply to.

What’s collected when you use drafft

  • Approximate area: with your permission, the app reads your location at reduced accuracy and rounds it on your phone to a grid of about 1 km before sending it. Your exact location never leaves your phone.
  • Device and connection: model, iOS and app versions, language, time zone, install identifier, IP address and the country it indicates, an Apple DeviceCheck token and a notification token.
  • Activity: when you were last active, notification settings, purchases and credits (boosts, super likes, drafft tempo). We never receive your card details: payments go through Apple.

What your phone’s permissions are used for

Camera, microphone and photo library let you add photos, a video or voice messages. The calendar is only used to add a session when you ask. You can withdraw each permission in iOS Settings. drafft doesn’t ask for your contacts, your health data or permission to track you.

Sensitive data

Your gender and the genders you want to see can reveal your sexual orientation. Your lifestyle answers (drinking, smoking, diet) can reveal information about your health or beliefs. Using a dating app is itself personal information.

This data is processed on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by filling it in so drafft can show you profiles and show yours. You can withdraw it at any time: edit or clear your lifestyle answers in your profile, or delete your account. Your gender and the genders you want to see are needed for drafft to work: to stop their processing, delete your account.

Automated processing

  • Order of profiles in Discover: it takes into account distance, recent activity, shared sports and whether someone has already liked you. It has no legal effect and doesn’t deny you any service.
  • Photo and video checks: every profile media and every photo sent in a chat is analysed automatically to detect nudity, violence or other prohibited content. A media can be rejected automatically; unclear cases are reviewed by a person. If you think a rejection is a mistake, write to us: a person will look at it again.
  • Banned accounts coming back: encrypted fingerprints of the email, number and device identifier help spot a banned account that returns. A match always leads to a review by a person, never to an automatic ban.
  • Phone number: we check the type of number; numbers that aren’t mobile numbers are refused.

What other members see

Other members see your profile: first name, age, gender, pronouns if you add them, neighbourhood, distance rounded to the kilometre (1 km minimum), bio, goal, favourite spot, the lifestyle answers you gave, sports, prompt answers, icebreaker, and approved photos, video and voice intro. The people you match with also see your messages and your session invites.

Never shown: your date of birth, email address, phone number and the genders you want to see. When you pause your profile, it no longer appears in Discover; your matches and chats stay.

Access by the drafft team

Only the team members who need it can access data, with rights limited to their role. To handle a report or look into a risk to members’ safety, moderation can read the chats involved and view a verification selfie. Every access is logged with its reason.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, receive it in a reusable format (portability) and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also set instructions for what happens to your data after your death.

  • Export your data: You › Privacy & data › Export my data. You get a link by email, usually within 24 hours, valid for 7 days.
  • Delete your account: You › Delete account. Deletion is immediate.
  • Edit your profile: at any time from the You tab.
  • Anything else: write to {{@privacyEmail}}. We reply within one month at most. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

If you think your rights aren’t being respected, you can complain to the CNIL (Commission nationale de l’informatique et des libertés, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France, cnil.fr) or to the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Traffic between you and the app or website is encrypted (HTTPS). Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers. Each member can only access what concerns them. Photos, videos and voice messages are private and served through temporary links. Team access is limited and logged. No measure is foolproof: if a data breach puts you at risk, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Under 18

drafft is only for people aged 18 or over. Age is checked at sign-up and the date of birth can’t be changed afterwards. If you think someone under 18 is using drafft, report their profile: the account will be closed and its data erased.

Cookies and trackers

The getdrafft.com website sets no cookies, uses no audience measurement tool and loads no third-party service: its fonts are hosted on the site. Our host, Cloudflare, processes visitors’ IP addresses for as long as it takes to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks.

The app uses no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics. It stores on your phone what it needs to work (login, cached profiles and media).

Changes

This policy may change as drafft evolves. The date of the last update is at the top of the page. If there’s an important change, we’ll let you know in the app before it applies.
